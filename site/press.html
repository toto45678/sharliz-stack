About the game

Sharliz Tower is a one-tap stacking game. Players drop wobbly, jelly-bean shaped Sharliz on top of each other and try to build the tallest tower they can without it toppling over.

The adventure crosses 8 hand-crafted worlds, from a sunny farm and a busy city to a candy land, a frozen kingdom, a tropical ocean, a volcano and outer space. Every world ends with a giant boss, like Count Crowley, Queen Gumdrop and Kraken Inky, who attacks the tower while the player stacks fast enough to strike back.

Key features

  • Simple one-tap controls with a satisfying wobble and "Perfect!" drops
  • 80 levels across 8 colorful 3D worlds, each with its own music
  • 8 boss battles with signature attacks
  • Collectible Sharliz and silly hats, each hat with its own power
  • Daily gift, missions and a sticker album
  • No account, no personal data collected
