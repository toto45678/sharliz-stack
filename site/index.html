For parents

Made for kids and families. Here's what you'll want to know.

✅ No account needed🔒 No personal data collected👪 Parent gate on purchases
Is it free?

Yes. Sharliz Tower is free to download and play. There are optional in-game purchases, and every purchase is behind a parent gate.

Does the game collect my child's data?

No. There is no sign-up and no tracking. Game progress is saved only on your device.

Are there ads?

No pop-up ads in the game. Everything can be earned just by playing.

What age is it for?

Sharliz Tower is rated 9+ on the App Store. Younger kids love it too with a grown-up nearby: the controls are a single tap, and there is an easy mode.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once it's installed, the whole game plays offline, perfect for car rides and flights.

Which languages does it speak?

14 languages, including English, Hebrew, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. It starts in your phone's language.

Where can I get it?

It's out now, free on iPhone and iPad: get it on the App Store. Android is coming soon. Join the waitlist and we'll send you one email when it's on Google Play.
