Out now on the App Store!

Sharliz Tower is here, free on iPhone and iPad: 300 levels, 30 bosses, 4 seasons, buddies, stickers and arcade games. Get it on the App Store. Android is coming soon to Google Play.

Bobby, Lulu, Mimi, Timmy, Nunu and Rocky got their names and a shiny new look. Each one has a personality of their own, and you'll be seeing a lot more of them soon.

Coming in the next update

Four cozy rooms, 25 pieces of furniture and 225 designs to mix and match. Every piece gives your Sharliz a power, and there are 6 house styles, from a cozy cottage to a candy house.

The sticker album grew to over 400 stickers. The super-rare gold chapter can only be filled by finishing missions, so every sticker in it is earned, never bought.

Sling Shot and Who's in the Shadow? joined the arcade. Quick mini games for a break from building towers.
