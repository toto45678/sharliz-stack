Website privacy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. This page covers the Sharliz Tower website. The game itself has its own privacy policy: it collects no personal information.

What we collect

Only the email address you type into the waitlist form, if you choose to sign up. We ask that only people aged 16 or older sign up. Kids can ask a parent or guardian to sign up for them.

What we use it for

  • One email when Sharliz Tower launches, with the launch gift for early fans.
  • Occasional big news about the game, only if you stay subscribed.

We never sell or share your email with advertisers.

Where it is kept

Emails are stored by our email service provider, which sends the emails for us. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.

The mini demo

The demo on the home page saves your best score and your sound setting only in your own browser. Nothing from the demo is sent to us.

Visit statistics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we know roughly how many people visit and which pages they look at. It uses no cookies, does not track you across other websites and does not tell us who you are.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time, or write to hello@sharliztower.com and we will delete your email.
