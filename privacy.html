Privacy Policy – Sharliz Tower

Last updated: October 6, 2026

In short

The game does not collect personal information, does not require an account, shows no ads, and uses no tracking or analytics tools.

What is stored, and where

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, hats and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own policies. We never receive or store payment details; the store only tells the game that a purchase succeeded so the item can be delivered.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. For questions, contact the developer email shown on the game's store page.
